# Random Pictionary

A quick drawing-and-guessing game for coworkers on a video call, or friends and family sharing one screen.

## Play it

[fill in your github.io link]

You need one phone or computer with the game open, and 2 to 8 players.

## How to play

1. Pick how many players you have and tap Start.
2. The artist taps "Show my word" while everyone else looks away. On a call, the artist shares their screen.
3. The artist draws the word. No letters, no talking. Everyone else shouts guesses.
4. When someone guesses right, the artist taps that player's button. They get 1 point.
5. Each round lasts 60 seconds. After 6 rounds, the player with the most points wins.
